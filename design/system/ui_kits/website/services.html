Services

Every engagement is quoted after a first call. That call is free and takes 30 minutes, on video or in person if you’re in the metro.

What I do

  • IT operations assessment

    I spend about two weeks in your tickets, accounts, documentation and MSP reports. You get a written plan in priority order. You can act on it with me or without me.

  • Identity and access cleanup

    I match what HR says against what Active Directory and Entra ID say, then close the gaps. Ghost accounts go. Roles get simpler so access requests stop being guesswork.

  • Service desk and automation

    I make the queue visible, set targets people can hit, and write down how things get done. Then I automate the work that keeps coming back.
