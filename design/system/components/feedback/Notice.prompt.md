An inline notice or banner; use for a short aside that should stand apart from body text.

```jsx
<Notice>I’m based in Atlanta. I work on-site around the metro and remotely anywhere, and I’ll travel for the right project.</Notice>
<Notice status="warn">…</Notice>
```

- Neutral (teal dot) for information; warn for something the reader should act on.
- For form results use the form’s own `role="alert"` and `role="status"`, not Notice.
