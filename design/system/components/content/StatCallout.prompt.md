A before-to-after number with its caption; use for real results like “24 roles down to 7”.

```jsx
<StatCallout from="24" to="7" caption="In one business unit of a 3,000-person company I took 24 roles down to 7." />
```

- Only for numbers from real work. Never round or invent.
- The big figures are aria-hidden; the caption carries the meaning.
