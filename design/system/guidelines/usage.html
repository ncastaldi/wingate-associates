Do

  • One h1 per page, an h2 per section.
  • One primary button per view. It goes to the form.
  • Keep links underlined.
  • Visible labels, errors under the field.
  • Real numbers from real work in stat callouts.
  • Check at 375px, both schemes, keyboard only.

Don’t

  • Colours outside the tokens, or status colours as decoration.
  • Photos, stock imagery, gradients, fake charts.
  • Radii past 4px, shadows at rest.
  • Classes or utility frameworks.
  • Analytics, trackers, third-party scripts.
  • An email address or phone number.
